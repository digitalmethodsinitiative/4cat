{# The annotation fields of a dataset, as a row in its metadata box. Collapsed by default: the fields as badges, or an invitation to make one. The editor beneath is fetched by htmx on the first click - one click both opens the row and loads it, so a dataset page nobody annotates never pays for it. A dataset with no fields yet has nothing to show while the editor is closed, so the right-hand cell stays out of the way until there is either a field or an open editor. `hasFields()` reads the badges rather than a flag because they are swapped in out of band when fields are saved; it is re-evaluated whenever `open` changes, which is the only moment the answer can matter. Always present once the dataset is finished, whichever pane is showing: the fields describe the dataset, not the Explorer. Expects: dataset, annotation_fields, can_annotate #}
